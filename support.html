nonograms original

Support

nonograms original is a hand-crafted puzzle game: solve picture logic puzzles, reveal the artwork inside. It runs entirely on your device, offline, with no accounts and no ads.

Get help

For bug reports, questions, or feedback, reach out via github.com/lou1sr1ch. Including your device model and iOS version helps track things down faster.

Common questions

How do I reset my progress? Deleting the app removes all saved progress and settings. Reinstalling starts fresh.

Does it work offline? Yes - every puzzle, image, and sound ships inside the app. Airplane Mode is fully supported.

Where did my progress go after reinstalling? Progress is stored on-device only and is removed with the app by design - there are no accounts and no cloud saves.
