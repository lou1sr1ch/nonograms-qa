Puzzle editor

The retired in-app editor, recreated as a standalone drafting station. Values mode edits the solution itself (which cells are filled — the clues follow); the art rides along underneath at full strength on filled cells and 40% on empty ones. Art mode paints colors and never touches the solution: color a filled cell to set its picture color, an empty cell to set an accent that appears on the win reveal — everything at full strength. The brush switch: Drag paints every cell the pointer crosses (with gap-filling), Tap changes only the cell you click. A source image can sit beside the grid and be pixelified — each cell’s area of the image averaged into that cell’s color — or held over the grid (the peek button, or the Space key) to compare against the win screen. Classify runs the same solver engine as the fleet checker. Everything autosaves.

Classification

CLEAN = unique + fully line-solvable from empty (what the fleet checker wants with zero givens). NEEDS-GUESSING = unique but pure line-logic stalls; the givens draft rings the cells to pre-reveal so the seeded puzzle line-solves, greedy-minimal per the 2026-08-29 drafter. Fleet cap: 3 givens, filled cells only — a draft over 3 or with an empty cell means nudge the art. NON-UNIQUE = more than one filling satisfies the clues, which breaks the exact-match win check. Colors never affect classification — they ride on top of the solution. Classification runs on demand; big sparse boards can take a few seconds.
