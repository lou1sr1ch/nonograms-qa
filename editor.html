Puzzle editor

The retired in-app editor, recreated as a standalone drafting station. Draw a grid, watch the clues compute live, and classify with the same solver engine as the fleet checker (a faithful port of solvability_check.py, the Phase 2 baseline). A draft is shippable when it classifies CLEAN (unique + line-solvable from empty) or NEEDS-GUESSING with a givens draft of 3 or fewer filled cells. Everything autosaves in this browser.

Classification

CLEAN = unique + fully line-solvable from empty (what the fleet checker wants with zero givens). NEEDS-GUESSING = unique but pure line-logic stalls; the givens draft rings the cells to pre-reveal so the seeded puzzle line-solves, greedy-minimal per the 2026-08-29 drafter. Fleet cap: 3 givens, filled cells only — a draft over 3 or with an empty cell means nudge the art. NON-UNIQUE = more than one filling satisfies the clues, which breaks the exact-match win check. Classification runs on demand; big sparse boards can take a few seconds.
