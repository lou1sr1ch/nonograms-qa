Paintings batch 2: five license-verified candidates

Your round-20 ask: brainstorm five paintings we can verify licenses for, BEFORE you spend editor time on art. Every source below was pulled from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-07 and its public-domain status confirmed against the Commons license metadata (the PD tags are listed per card). Each candidate is preloaded in the editor (editor.html, group "Candidates - paintings batch 2") so you can touch up or redraw immediately. Two read well as auto-pixelated starters; three are weak on purpose and want a full editor pass or the bin. Grid, fact, and license audit are staged; nothing ships from this page without your ruling.

The ranking, honestly: sunflowers and wanderer are the strong reads (and both PORTRAIT 15x20, the aspect the fleet needs most). Great wave, the kiss, and the sleeping gypsy are staged for their licenses and subjects, not their starter grids. All five are unique and line-solve from empty, so any grid you end up with keeps that bar.
