Privacy Policy - effective 2026-09-07

The short version: nonograms original collects no data about you. Nothing leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your puzzle progress and settings are saved locally on your device, and nowhere else. This information is never transmitted, synced, or shared. Deleting the app deletes it completely.

What the app does not do

No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No third-party SDKs. No cookies. No crash-reporting services. The app makes no network requests of any kind and works fully in Airplane Mode.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL before the change ships.
