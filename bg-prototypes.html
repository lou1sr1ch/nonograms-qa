Background Motion Studies

Six live candidates for the per-category animated backgrounds — flora in its deep green + pastel scheme, fauna in orange-yellow with the walking paw prints. Each frame is a phone-shaped canvas with a mock puzzle list laid over it, so legibility is judged against something close to the real screen, not a bare animation.

Nothing on this page is in the app. Whichever study wins gets rebuilt inside it.

How to judge: watch each one for 20+ seconds before deciding. The right background is the one you stop noticing — if any of them pulls your eye away from the list rows, that's the one to kill. Everything is a dial: speed, density, size, opacity, palette.
